# Demo script: read this aloud (10 minutes)

Plain words, short. **DO** = what you do. Say the line, then do the thing.
Keep the presenter page open on a second screen: `http://127.0.0.1:8787/#/demo` (clock, same lines, one fallback button per scene).
Longer notes, checklist and questions: `docs/DEMO.md`.

---

**0:00 The problem.** DO: headphones on, music playing.
> "I work with headphones on. I forget to blink, I skip breaks, and nobody can warn me when someone walks up behind me.
> Desk Coach notices, on this computer only, and taps me through the devices I already use."

**0:45 How it works.**
> "The camera and the microphone notice things. One plugin inside Logi Options+ decides if it is worth interrupting me.
> Then my mouse buzzes, my keypad lights up, or something small shows on screen. Nothing leaves this laptop."

**1:30 You stay in control.** DO: press a key on the keypad, then flip a switch on the page.
> "Every feature has a switch, here and on the keypad. I press a key and that widget goes grey. I flip it here and the key changes.
> I choose how each one reaches me, and how often. In Presenting mode it goes silent for the audience."

**2:30 Speech.** DO: talk fast for 10 seconds, then very quietly for 5.
> "Talking too fast? A snail. Too quiet? A duck."

**3:30 Blinking.** DO: stare for 6 seconds, then blink.
> "At a screen I stop blinking. An owl reminds me."

**4:15 Pomodoro.** DO: press "1-minute demo timer", press the Pomodoro key, step away, come back.
> "A focus timer that only counts when I am really here. I step away, it pauses. I come back, it goes on."

**5:30 Volume when far.** DO: step back, leave the frame, come back.
> "Further from the screen, the sound gets louder. Out of the room, it mutes. When I am back, my volume returns."

**6:30 Person behind.** DO: headphones on. A colleague walks past, then walks up to you. Open the mouse ring.
> "With headphones on I cannot hear anyone. Someone walks past: nothing, we ignore that.
> Someone walks up to me: my mouse buzzes and the keypad flashes. From the mouse ring I can lower the music, hide my screen, or say it is fine."

**8:30 Brightness.** DO: open the grey "Screen brightness" card.
> "Brightness is a concept. The camera does not give us brightness, so this has to be switched on in the camera hardware.
> With a camera that does, we would adjust the monitor, the keyboard light and the room lights."

**9:15 Close.**
> "Local, quiet, and built on your devices. We would love: brightness from the camera, a second person seen by the camera,
> a settings panel inside Options+, haptic length, and opening the ring from a plugin. Thank you."

---

**If something does not work:** press the grey **Fallback** button for that part on the Demo page and say "here I trigger it by hand".
**If you run late:** skip "Volume when far", then "Blinking".
